[TEAM BATTLE RULES & INFORMATION]
Overview:

The Weekly Accountability Team Battle is a one-week study competition between two teams. Participants have individual weekly study-hour targets and weekly goals. The two teams compete based on their total study hours, but individual participants must also complete their declared goals and weekly hour targets.

Rules:
  1. Participants create a weekly to-do list and declare their study-hour targets before the challenge.
  2. Study hours are tracked using YPT.
  3. The two teams compete based on their combined total study hours.
  4. Completing individual weekly goals and declared study-hour targets is mandatory.
  5. A participant who fails their weekly goals or target study hours receives the community's punishment pfp according to the accountability rules.
